## Joshua 3:14

**Verse 14.** “And all the people departed from their tents.” That is, like the filling of a threshing floor. For it was not only made firm, as at the Red Sea, but was heaped up like a mound of wheat—plainly as a type of the apostles.

<!-- source-locus: 6.15 -->
## Joshua 6:15–16

**Verses 15–16.** “Cry out, for the Lord has handed the city over to you.” Why did he command that Jericho be put under the ban? This was the first city they took after crossing the river. Just as he commanded them to offer the firstfruits of their crops, so he ordered them to present the first offerings from the spoils. Besides these things, he also trains them through these laws so that they keep the laws that are necessary. And, displaying his own power to them, he caused them to take the first city without weapons or siege engines and destroyed it by the sound of the trumpets alone. Thus, whenever they should be defeated while drawn up for battle, they might know that they themselves had supplied the causes of their defeat by despising the divine laws.

<!-- source-locus: 8.2 -->
## Joshua 8:2

**Verse 2.** “And you shall take the plunder of the livestock as plunder for yourself.” For it was not necessary that everything be devoted to destruction as at Jericho.

<!-- source-locus: 8.26 -->
## Joshua 8:26

**Verse 26.** “And Joshua stretched out the javelin, and his hand.” For Joshua remained with his hand extended so that, when those in the ambush saw it, they might move first to seize the city.
