## Judges 15:19

**Verse 19.** “God split open the hollow place in the jaw, and water flowed from it.” In other words, God split the ground at the cleft called “the Jaw,” and water flowed out.

## Judges 20:21

**Verse 21.** “The Benjaminites came out.” Why were the Israelites defeated when their cause for attacking was just? Clearly, earlier offenses still counted against them. As the psalm says, “When I choose the appointed time, I will judge with equity.” After Israel had been defeated once and then a second time, divine wrath turned against the wrongdoers, and the Israelites won a just victory.

What deserves admiration is that the mass of the Israelites had undertaken a just campaign against Benjamin, yet when they were defeated they entertained no wicked thought about God. They continued to weep before him until judgment finally fell on those who had done wrong. They did not reproach God or demand that what they had already done should be enough; they begged him to show them the right way to proceed.

This is where the saying is fulfilled: “Benjamin is a ravenous wolf; in the morning he will devour, and in the evening he will distribute food.” Benjamin first won the battle, but in the end the tribe itself became food for the people.
