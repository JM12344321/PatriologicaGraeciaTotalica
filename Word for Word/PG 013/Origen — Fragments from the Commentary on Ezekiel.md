## Ezekiel 30.25

1. Codex 221a. Chapter XXX.25. The arms of Pharaoh are wickedness in the practice of sin and, in doctrines of impiety, the mind-set of the flesh. God, because he is good, shatters these arms. Pharaoh is a symbol of the devil, the ruler of sin among human beings. —The Lord shatters the arm of sinners so that they may not be able to take hold of a sword.

## Ezekiel 32.6

2. Codex folio 229a. XXXII.6. And this, above all, shows the allegory of Scripture: Pharaoh, bodily speaking, would not water the earth from his excretions; rather, the dung-like discharge of the adversary waters earthly things.

## Ezekiel 32.23

3. Codex folio 222b. XXXII.23. The Hebrew [text] has “in the length of a pit.” For *iarēch*, pronounced with rough breathing according to the language of the Hebrews, signifies both “thigh” and “length.” With reference to the synagogue, it has the extended sense [of length]. Therefore it calls the depth of the burial a pit, and the extent of the burial its length.

## Ezekiel 44.1

4. Codex folio 232b. XLIV.1. Ezekiel, the son of man, describes many gates and has related something concerning each one. But now he speaks, to those having ears, concerning the gate of the saints that looks toward the east, about which it is written that this gate shall be shut, and what follows. —It is shut not only because the Lord, the God of Israel, passes through the gate, but also because the ruler sits in that gate to eat before the Lord by the way of Ailam, which is interpreted “a gate’s vestibule.” —The closed gate [signifies] the mysteries of God, which cannot be shut up. —Knowledge has a key, as the Lord says to the lawyers. Therefore the gate is shut to human beings, for the things within the gate are what eye has not seen and ear has not heard, and what follows. —The sealed book written about in Isaiah has a meaning like that of this gate. You will find this same book more clearly also in the Apocalypse of John, together with the things written about it: how the one from the tribe of Judah opened these things—and he alone. For until our Lord Jesus Christ came, the law, the prophetic word, was shut; and the stone of hardening was a covering over these things, which Jacob removed from the well. For in many places Scripture has opened the meaning concerning this gate.
