## Numbers 11:7

## Numbers 24:7
<!-- source-locus: 24.7 -->

**Verse 7.** “He shall rule over many nations.” There are many lords and many gods, but those who have Jesus Christ as their Lord are blessed. The final clause says they will rule over many `κυρίας`—literally feminine “ladies” or “mistresses”—an odd reading that should not be normalized without another witness.

## Numbers 24:17
<!-- source-locus: 24.17 -->

**Verse 17.** “A man shall arise from Israel.” The note reports a tradition that Balaam had students in magic, that his prophecies—including “A star shall arise”—were written down and left behind, and that the Magi may have received this material through ancestral tradition and succession before coming to Bethlehem. These are reported possibilities in a text whose linking phrase is elliptical, not independent historical claims.
