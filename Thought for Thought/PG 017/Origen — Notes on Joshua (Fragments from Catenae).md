## Joshua 3:14

**Verse 14.** “All the people left their tents.” The water filled up like grain covering a threshing floor. It did not merely stand still, as at the Red Sea; it piled up like a mound of wheat. This clearly prefigured the apostles.

<!-- source-locus: 6.15 -->
## Joshua 6:15–16

**Verses 15–16.** “Raise the battle cry, for the Lord has given you the city.” Why did God command that Jericho be put under the ban? It was the first city the Israelites captured after crossing the river. Just as he commanded them to offer the firstfruits of their crops, he required them to present the first spoils as an offering. These commands also trained them to obey the indispensable laws. God demonstrated his power by enabling them to capture their first city without weapons or siege engines: the sound of trumpets alone brought it down. If they were later defeated in battle, they would therefore know that they had brought the defeat on themselves by disregarding God’s laws.

<!-- source-locus: 8.2 -->
## Joshua 8:2

**Verse 2.** “You may take the livestock as plunder for yourselves.” Unlike Jericho, not everything here had to be devoted to destruction.

<!-- source-locus: 8.26 -->
## Joshua 8:26

**Verse 26.** “Joshua stretched out the javelin and his hand.” Joshua kept his hand extended as a signal, so that the troops lying in ambush would see it and rush ahead to capture the city.
