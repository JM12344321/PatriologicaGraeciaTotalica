## Corrections and Additions

It should be noted:

1. That all the notes occurring throughout the *Apologeticum* which are not signed with an author’s name are to be referred to Havercamp’s continuous commentary.

2. That the variant readings cited in nearly every column from the Paris edition were taken from the first Paris edition, prepared by Sigismund Gelenius, 1566, with a very few exceptions, which the editors drew from Priorius’s Paris edition of 1664.

3. That, because of the excessive number of Tertullian’s works and the enormous bulk of both volumes, there was so little space available for indexes that the lexicon of Tertullian’s Latin could scarcely be placed at the end of the second volume; the very extensive index of all subjects, however, had to be deferred to the third volume, which it will conclude.

## The Following Additions Are to Be Made to the Literary Annals of Tertullian

[Col. 1335]

### Year 1702

The Blessed Cardinal Joseph Maria Thomasius published the first volume of his *Theological Institutions*, at the beginning of which he printed Tertullian’s book *On Prescriptions against Heretics*. Rome, quarto.

### Year 1769

When Anton Francesco Vezzosi, C.R., revised and republished those same *Theological Institutions of the Ancient Fathers* by the venerable Blessed Thomasius, the book *On Prescriptions* was once again issued under his supervision, enlarged with several notes displaying sound doctrine and exceptional learning. Rome, from the printing house of Marco Palearini. Quarto.

### Year 1840

Among certain principal minor works of ecclesiastical writers, [Col. 1336] Martin Joseph Routh, S.T.P., President of St Mary Magdalen College, Oxford, published two books by Tertullian—namely, *On Prayer* and *On the Prescription of Heretics*—and furnished them with notes both by others and by himself. He also augmented them with a new collection of readings, placed at the end and taken from an Ambrosian manuscript. Second edition, Oxford. It is regrettable that this excellent work did not come into our hands until the whole of this present work had been completed; nevertheless, we shall give it such notice as we can in the third volume, in the index.

### Year 1843

We are able to report only that, at the same time as this edition of ours is being committed to the press, another edition of Tertullian is likewise beginning to appear, edited by H. A. Woodham, Cambridge.

[Col. 1135] A very extensive analytical index will conclude the third volume.
