## Exodus 15:25

**Verse 25.** “There God set statutes and judgments for him, and there he tested him.” God tests for benefit, for he is himself incapable of being tempted by evils. But he tests for this reason: so that, if they obey him, they may obtain good things and be released from evils.

## Exodus 20:4

**Verse 4.** “You shall not make for yourself an idol.” An idol is one thing, and a likeness another. It is a likeness if, either through craftsmanship or through painting, you make a likeness of a fish, a four-footed animal, or a wild beast. An idol, however, is whatever the soul, giving shape to an image, makes from things that do not exist as originals—for example, some living creature compounded from a human being and a horse.

**Verse 4.** “Whatever is in heaven above, whatever is on the earth below, and whatever is in the waters beneath the earth: you shall not bow down to them, nor shall you serve them.” To bow down is one thing, and to serve another. The person who serves these things with his whole soul not only bows down but also renders them sacred service. The person who acts under pretense and does it because of custom does not serve, but he does bow down. You will find such people in Numbers, gratifying women and bowing down to idols.

## Exodus 28:30

**Verse 30.** Since it befits the high priest to carry before and behind him, like a shield, protective vigilance, lest some hostile counsel of the spirits of wickedness wound the rational faculty, throw the proven soundness of judgments into confusion, and, through an admixture of evil, cause Manifestation and Truth to depart.

[Translation note: “Manifestation and Truth” renders the Greek names used in this cultic context for the objects placed in the breastpiece of judgment.]

## Exodus 31:1

**Verse 1.** “And the Lord spoke to Moses.” For all of forty days the all-wise God kept Moses in order to give the law, although he was able to give the law in three days. But this was so that the time of Moses’ absence might expose the ungodliness hidden in the depth of the Jews’ heart.
