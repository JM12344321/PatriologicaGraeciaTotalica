For the very compositions of your poets are memorials of frenzy and lack of self-control. For one who attends the man pre-eminent among you in education is more miserable than all human beings. First, they say that Agamemnon, cooperating with his brother’s intensified lack of self-control, frenzy, and unrestrained desire, consented to give his daughter for sacrifice and to throw all Greece into turmoil, in order that he might rescue Helen, carried off from a leprous shepherd. And when, after the war had prevailed, they brought captives, Agamemnon himself was being led captive by Chryseis; because of Briseis he raised enmity against the child of Thetis. —who killed a lion and destroyed the many-headed hydra; who slew an untiring wild beast, was able to bring down man-eating flying birds, brought the three-headed dog up from Hades, and was able to demolish Augeias’s strong wall of dung; who killed bulls and a doe whose nostrils breathed fire; who obtained golden fruit from a stem; who killed a venom-spitting serpent and Achelous—why he killed them it is not lawful to say—and the guest-killing Busiris; who leaped mountains in order to obtain water giving articulate speech, as the story says: he who was able to do things so many, so great, and so vast, like a child was pounded with cymbals by satyrs; defeated by womanish love, he took pleasure in being struck on the buttocks by a laughing Lydian. Let Hephaestus put aside jealousy and not begrudge it if, old and lame, he was hated in his foot, while Ares, young and handsome, was loved. Since therefore, men of Greece, your gods have been exposed by lack of self-control and your heroes as unmanly—as the dramatic stories among you have shown: Atreus’s pollutions, Thyestes’ beds, the abominations of Pelops’ descendants, Danaus killing through jealousy and becoming drunk childless, while the Furies prepared the Thyestean banquets—Procne, even now winged, wails, and her tongue-cut sister, the Cecropian, chirps. Why need one even mention Oedipus’s goads: Laius’s murder, marriage to his mother, and the mutual slaughter at once of his brothers and children? I have also hated your festivals.
