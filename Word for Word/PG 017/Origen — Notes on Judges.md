## Judges 15:19

**Verse 19.** “And God split open the wound that was in the jaw, and water came out from it.” This stands in place of: “By the cleaving of the jawbone he split the ground, and waters came out.”

## Judges 20:21

**Verse 21.** “And the sons of Benjamin came out.” Why are those who went out against them justly defeated? Plainly it was because of causes that preceded this. The psalm also says, “When I take the appointed time, I will judge uprightly.” Then it says: after they had been defeated both once and a second time, wrath passed over to those who had done wrong, and the Israelites prevailed with a just judgment.

It is worthy of wonder that the multitude of the Israelites, although they had gone against the Benjaminites justly and had been defeated, conceived nothing evil against God. Rather, they did not cease weeping before him until they obtained judgment against those who had done wrong. They did not say, blaming God, “But this is enough”; they begged to learn the proper course.

“Benjamin is a ravening wolf; in the morning he will eat, and [in the evening] he will distribute food” is fulfilled here. For after conquering at first, he became food for the people at the end.
